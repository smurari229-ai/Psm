# DukaanBoost — First Customer Validation

This is the next milestone after the MVP deploy: verify that real local businesses find the tool useful before spending money or adding paid APIs.

## Current product truth
- Live site: https://dukaanboost.vercel.app
- The current MVP generates template-based captions, WhatsApp promotional copy, short ad lines, and a 7-day posting plan.
- It is not an AI-backed generator yet. Do not promise AI, automatic posting, guaranteed reach, or guaranteed sales.
- Do not collect customer passwords, payment details, or private customer lists during validation.

## Goal for the first 7 days
Speak to 5–10 local businesses and learn:
1. Can the owner generate and copy useful content on their phone?
2. Would they use it again next week?
3. Which output saves them the most time?
4. Would they pay ₹99–₹199 per month, and what would they expect for that price?

A verbal compliment is not proof of willingness to pay. Record actual use, repeat use, and any explicit purchase intent.

## Best first segment
Start with one segment only: salons/barbers. They regularly promote services, offers, and seasonal deals, and the sample can be prepared without customer data. If access to this segment is difficult, test one alternative segment (for example, home bakers) but do not mix all segments in the first batch.

## Mobile-first test
1. Open the live site on your phone.
2. Choose the closest business category and Hindi/Hinglish language.
3. Enter a real, non-sensitive offer (for example, “weekday haircut offer”).
4. Generate a caption and WhatsApp message.
5. Copy the result and show the owner how they could edit it.
6. Ask the owner to try the tool themselves, without taking their phone or account details.
7. Ask for honest feedback and permission before quoting their comments publicly.

## Questions to ask after the demo
- Kya aap is message ko apne business ke liye use karenge? Kyon/kyon nahi?
- Isme sabse useful aur sabse confusing cheez kya thi?
- Aap abhi aise captions/messages kaise banate hain?
- Kya aap agle hafte dobara use karenge?
- Agar yeh aapka time bachaye, to ₹99/month par try karenge? Agar nahi, to kis wajah se?

Do not pressure people to say yes. “No” is useful evidence.

## Decision rule after 5 tests
- If 3 or more owners can use it and copy output without help, keep improving usability based on their exact problems.
- If 2 or more independently ask to use it again, test a repeat-use flow before building accounts or payments.
- If nobody would pay, do not add a payment system yet. Ask what outcome they would pay for and revise the offer.
- If owners need custom designs/posters more than text, consider offering a small manual service first to test demand before automating it.

These are practical decision thresholds, not market statistics or an income forecast.

## Do not spend yet
No paid ads, domain purchase, AI API subscription, payment integration, or large feature rewrite until at least a few real owners demonstrate repeated use or clear willingness to pay.

## Definition of success
The first milestone is not “the site is online.” It is evidence from real business owners: successful self-service use, useful copied output, repeat use, and ideally a real paying customer. Revenue is not yet verified.
